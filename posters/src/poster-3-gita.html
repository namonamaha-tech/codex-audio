Read it like an entrepreneur

Gītā × Thyrocare
{{icon:tabler-briefcase}}

A business is a resolve

The Gita's word for resolve, vyavasāya, is the everyday Hindi word for business. By its name, a venture is a decision you stick to.

{{icon:tabler-git-branch}}

Many branches = endless

Ten side-projects each get a tenth of your time and money. None finishes. Krishna's word for that mind: ananta, endless.

{{icon:tabler-plant-2}}

Trunk first, crown later

Velumani: succeed first in one thing, then diversify. Branches grow from a strong trunk; a bush never becomes a tree.

{{icon:tabler-microscope}}

The proof is in the name

Thyro-care began with thyroid tests alone, scaled that one thing nationwide, and only then branched into wider diagnostics.
