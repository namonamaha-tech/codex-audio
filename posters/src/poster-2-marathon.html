01

Pace yourself

Sprinters burst and fade. Those who rush find it hard to sustain success. Pick a pace you can hold for years.

02

Let it compound

Thyrocare grew about 25% a year for 25 years. No magic — just the same good stride, year after year.

03

Show up at 5 a.m.

“Define priorities and focus from 5:00 am, consistently.” Stamina is built daily, not on race day.

04

Run light, run one route

No debt weighing you down, no four rabbits to chase. He built Thyrocare debt-free, on one problem.
