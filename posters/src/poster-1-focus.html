The Core Philosophy

Focus vs. Diversification 3 rules
01

Monotask to scale

Do one thing and you can scale it everywhere. Do everything and you stay stuck in one place.

02

Beware the distraction trap

Running a dozen operations? Name the one that brings the most happiness, profit & growth. Can't pick one? That isn't diversification — it's distraction.

03

Follow the sequence →

Succeed first at one single thing. Only then diversify. Never the other way round.
